## Source limitations

The standard 2-bedroom and 2-bedroom End pages use the same illustration and floor-plan image. The End-unit page explicitly identifies this shared drawing as an unverified End-unit layout. A distinct 58.73 sqm drawing was not found. The original drawings do not print unit areas or dimensions and appear soft despite their large pixel dimensions; no detail was invented or reconstructed. Shared furnished-gallery images are shown at development level because the source does not map them to specific units. Renderings are labeled as marketing illustrations rather than actual completed-unit photos. Parking is not assumed to be included with a unit.

The reference site identifies itself as an accredited seller's website, with smdc.com as the official developer website. Media is attributed by that source to SMDC; no ownership or redistribution license is asserted. The source's training-only brochure and AI-generated homepage header were excluded. Review source media terms before publication.
