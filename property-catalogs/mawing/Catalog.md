## Project features and financing

Advertised amenities in seminar page 7: clubhouse, basketball court, parks and jogging path. Current completion, operation, fees and access require confirmation.

Supplied inclusions advertise furniture, appliances and landscaping. Model photographs show varying fit-out stages. Exact turnover inclusions require a written offer.

Seminar page 19 lists cash and bank financing. Pag-IBIG is marked on process; it is not represented as approved or available. Lot-specific payment schedules and discount arrangements are withheld pending confidentiality review.

The source completion wording is Project Completion Date: L.D. December 2030. It does not establish the construction or turnover date of a particular house.

Source developer material states DHSUD LTS No. 0003010 (04Sep2025). Independent regulator verification was not performed.

## Files and original media

The JSON database contains project, models, pricing, amenities, media, verification and a source-review ledger. The workbook has eight searchable sheets. Published images and original marketing images are in `public/images/mawing`; generic source PDFs are in `public/documents/mawing`.

Original JPG/PNG marketing files are retained byte for byte, except one duplicate shares the same output file. All 47 HEIC photographs contained GPS metadata. Their full-resolution JPEG conversions omit EXIF/GPS; original HEIC files remain in the supplied input folder. No image content or floor-plan artwork was redrawn.

The full seminar is withheld because it combines safe material with inventory-related/site and individual pricing content. Only the reviewed public marketing pages have been exported as full-page images.
