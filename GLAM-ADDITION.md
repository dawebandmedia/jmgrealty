## Important source conflicts and omissions

The balcony model cards state 26.82 sqm and a rounded PHP7.8M figure, while the newer calculation uses 30.23 sqm/PHP7,592,000. The listing explicitly exposes this disagreement. The one-bedroom End page uses a drawing printed 26.82 sqm despite its 33.30 sqm computation. Its `airmu1` illustration has uncertain Glam provenance and was excluded. That unit's card uses a clearly labeled **development exterior rendering**; it is not presented as an end-unit interior.

SMDC's official overview states a PHP5.7M–13.9M headline range without mapping prices to unit types. The seller's homepage has an older October2024 range. Neither is treated as a replacement for the labeled historical model computations. The developer page's abbreviated unit list shows one-bedroom, while its September2025 article supports one- and two-bedroom homes; the four detailed source variants remain labeled seller examples.

The seller's February28,2024 target date does not establish actual completion. Its footer/contact license numbers differ; no verified license or completion counter was created. Nearby destinations are sourced, but numerical travel times and an unverified map pin are not asserted. Google Maps is a location-search link; no source API key was copied.

No distinct amenity picture was verified for the kiddie pool, commercial strip, kid's play area or Jacuzzi. These facilities are listed with source context; no fabricated image was substituted. Shared interior renderings do not establish which of the four unit variants they represent. Small or soft plan labels were not reconstructed.
