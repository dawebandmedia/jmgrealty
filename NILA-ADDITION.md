# Nila Residences addition

Added 15 October 2026 under Metro Manila → Manila → Paco.

## Included

- One Nila development guide with four unit types: Premium 3 Bedroom, Signature 3 Bedroom, Premium 4 Bedroom and Garden 4 Bedroom.
- Four matching generic floor-plan sheets, preserving their original labels, dimensions and geometry. The image viewer supports enlargement, zoom and opening the original-resolution image.
- Eleven Mont architectural renderings: six shared interior perspectives and five exterior, courtyard or pool perspectives. Renderings remain clearly labeled; no type-specific photograph association is claimed.
- A separate amenities section and official source links, location context and viewing/price inquiry links.
- Generic VAT-inclusive price ranges dated 23 June 2026. Transfer and other charges are excluded; numeric budget matching uses the lower range bound. These historical guides do not replace a current quotation.
- Catalog filters, model detail pages, saved-model comparison and inquiry context for all four types.
- Higher budget choices of ₱100M, ₱150M and ₱200M in the catalog.

## Verification

TypeScript checking and the production build passed. Nineteen new and existing page routes were checked, along with all 30 Nila original/display image files and their original checksums. ## Source differences kept visible

The developer’s Premium 4 type overview says approximately 370.25 sqm; its standard generic drawing totals 352.50 sqm. The Garden 4 drawing totals 388 sqm and includes a labeled 35.50 sqm garden. The drawing shows three parking spaces, while the developer’s separate Unit C2 summary says two. Exact sizes, parking, outdoor spaces and finishes depend on the offered subtype.

## Privacy

Only selected generic marketing images and unit-type drawings were added as website assets. No inventory spreadsheets or statuses, unit-by-unit price schedules, client-specific computations, buyer documents, reservation forms, identity documents or commission materials were copied into this delivery. The older standalone property-inventory CSV is also omitted from the delivered ZIP; the public catalog remains intact.
